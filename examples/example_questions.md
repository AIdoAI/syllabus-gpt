# Example Questions for Syllabus-GPT

Use these questions to test Syllabus-GPT with your course materials.

## General Course Information

- What are the course prerequisites?
- Who is the instructor and what are their office hours?
- What textbooks are required for this course?
- What is the course description?
- Are there any recommended readings?

## Grading and Assessment

- What is the grading breakdown for this course?
- How much is the final exam worth?
- What is the late submission policy?
- How is participation graded?
- What are the criteria for getting an A?
- Is there a curve in this class?

## Schedule and Deadlines

- When is the midterm exam?
- What date is the final project due?
- When are assignment submissions typically due?
- What topics are covered in week 5?
- When is the last day to drop the course?

## Assignments and Projects

- What types of assignments are there?
- How many problem sets are there?
- What is the group project about?
- Are there any extra credit opportunities?
- What format should assignments be submitted in?

## Policies

- What is the attendance policy?
- What is the academic integrity policy?
- Can I use AI tools for assignments?
- What happens if I miss an exam?
- Is there a makeup exam policy?

## Technical Details

- What programming language will we use?
- What software do I need to install?
- Are there any lab sessions?
- What are the technical prerequisites?

## Advanced Queries

- Compare the grading policies across my uploaded syllabi
- What are all the exam dates across my courses?
- Which courses have group projects?
- What are the common themes across all course descriptions?
- Which course has the most flexible late policy?

## Tips for Good Questions

✅ **Be Specific**: "What is the weight of homework assignments?" rather than "Tell me about homework"

✅ **Ask One Thing**: "When is the final exam?" rather than "Tell me everything about exams"

✅ **Use Course Terms**: If the syllabus calls them "problem sets", use that term

✅ **Follow Up**: Build on previous answers with related questions

❌ **Avoid**: Very broad questions like "Tell me everything about this course"

## Testing Source Citations

Try these to verify citations work correctly:

1. Ask a question you know the answer to
2. Check if the source citation matches where you know the info is
3. Verify page numbers are correct
4. Test with multiple documents to see citation grouping
